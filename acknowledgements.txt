Floatcut is based on Flycut, developed by General Arcade (http://www.generalarcade.com/). Flycut is a fork of Jumpcut. Original source code available on GitHub: http://github.com/TermiT/Flycut
This fork was updated by haad to compile on Tahoe 26.0 release of Mac OS.
Thanks to haad (https://github.com/haad), whose Flycut fork provided the foundation this application builds upon.
